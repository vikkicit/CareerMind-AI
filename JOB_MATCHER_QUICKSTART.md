# 🎯 Job Matcher - Quick Start Guide

## 🚀 Get Started in 3 Minutes

### Step 1: Navigate to Job Matcher
- Open the AI Academic Assistant
- Click **🎯 Job Matcher** in the sidebar

### Step 2: Upload Your Resume
1. Click **"Choose a resume PDF"** button
2. Select your resume file
3. Wait for ✅ confirmation

### Step 3: Paste Job Description
1. Copy the entire job description (from LinkedIn, Indeed, etc.)
2. Paste into the text area
3. No need to edit - paste as-is

### Step 4: Click Analyze
1. Click **🚀 Analyze Match** button
2. Wait for AI analysis (~10-20 seconds)
3. Results appear automatically

## 📊 Understanding Your Results

### Match Score Card
```
🟢 Job Match Score: 78%
Status: Good
```
- **🟢 75-100%:** Excellent - You're a strong candidate
- **🟡 50-75%:** Good - Missing some skills, but fixable
- **🔴 0-50%:** Needs Improvement - Significant gaps

### Matching Skills Section
Shows skills you already have that the job requires:
- ✅ Python
- ✅ Machine Learning
- ✅ SQL

### Missing Skills Section
Shows skills required by the job that you don't have:
- ❌ Docker
- ❌ AWS
- ❌ Kubernetes

## 💡 Getting AI Recommendations

### Available Tabs:
1. **💡 Improvements** - What to focus on
2. **🛠️ Projects** - Projects to build (with tech details)

Click any tab to expand and read the full recommendation.

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: How accurate is the match score?**
A: The score combines skill matching (60%) and text analysis (40%). It's a good indicator but not definitive. Human review is still important.

**Q: Can I use the same resume multiple times?**
A: Yes! Analyze different jobs with the same resume to identify skill gaps.

**Q: How long does analysis take?**
A: Usually 10-20 seconds depending on resume and job description length.

**Q: What if my resume fails to upload?**
A: Ensure the PDF is valid and readable. Try opening it normally first.

**Q: Can I upload multiple resumes?**
A: Currently, one at a time. You can re-upload to analyze against different jobs.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: Yes! All processing is local. Your data stays on your machine.

**Q: Can I export the analysis?**
A: Currently displayed in-browser. Copy-paste sections or take screenshots.
